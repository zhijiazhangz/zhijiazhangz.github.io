Matroids and the Integral Hodge Conjecture

This is an algebraic geometry learning seminar (2026 Fall) on matroids, degenerations of abelian varieties, and their applications to the integral Hodge conjecture, stable rationality, and complex structures on S6.

Time: Fridays, 1:30–3:00 pm
Location: Room 507, Mathematics Hall
Organizers: James Hotchkiss, Daigo Ito, Yoonjoo Kim, and Zhijia Zhang

References

  1. [EFS] Philip Engel, Olivier de Gaay Fortman, and Stefan Schreieder, Combinatorics and Hodge theory of degenerations of abelian varieties: A survey of the Mumford construction.
  2. [EFS2] Philip Engel, Olivier de Gaay Fortman, and Stefan Schreieder, Matroids and the integral Hodge conjecture for abelian varieties.
  3. [E] Philip Engel, Complex structures on S6.

Schedule

  • September 18: Introductions (Speaker: James Hotchkiss)
  • September 25:
    (§2 [EFS]) Backgrounds on degenerations of abelian varieties (Speaker: Yoonjoo Kim)
    Abstract

    We will learn/review some basic material for our discussion of matroidal degenerations of abelian varieties. This includes principally polarized abelian varieties (ppav), Hodge structures of weight -1, log monodromies, and toroidal compactifications of A_g associated to certain cones B of positive-definite symmetric matrices / Q.

  • October 2: No meeting
  • October 9:
    (§3 [EFS]) Mumford construction over a polydisk (Speaker: Daigo Ito)
    Abstract

    We will first explain the basic idea of the Mumford construction via equivariant toric extensions of intermediate covers, and then describe the construction over a polydisk. We will also briefly discuss its connection with toroidal compactifications of A_g.
